## Acceptable use

You must not, and must not let anyone else:

1. copy, modify, decompile, disassemble or reverse engineer the Service or any part of
   it, or try to derive its source code, data models, prompts or algorithms, except to
   the extent the law expressly allows despite this restriction;
2. access, scrape, crawl, index or download the Service or its content by any automated
   means (bots, scripts, crawlers, AI agents or browsing agents), other than through the
   API or MCP connector with a key issued to you and within their documented limits;
3. use the Service, its output or its documentation to build, train, fine-tune or
   evaluate a product, service or machine-learning model that competes with the
   Service;
4. extract the Service's system prompts, instructions or internal documents from its AI
   features, or try to make those features act outside their purpose;
5. probe, scan or test the Service's security, or work around its rate limits, usage
   limits or access controls, without our written permission;
6. resell, sublicense or provide the Service to third parties except as these Terms
   allow.

## Our response

We may monitor use of the Service for breaches of this section, and may limit, suspend
or end access, and keep the evidence, if we reasonably believe one has happened.
